🎯 X-9 INTERCEPTOR SIM

The X-9 lifts off slowly and builds vertical speed, eases off as it pitches over into forward flight, then accelerates hard toward cruise. Approaching the target it backs off the throttle, locks on, and sprints into the block at maximum velocity.

Use the camera modes on the right to fly with it from as close as 2 m — drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

The nose-camera feed (bottom-left) is the live seeker: it finds the target, locks on, and steers the terminal dive from what it sees.

FLIGHT PROFILE & CONTROLS

Speed profile

  • 1 · Vertical ascent — starts from zero with a very gentle acceleration ramp, gradually building climb speed; eases off before the top.
  • 2 · Transition — the nose pitches from vertical to level on a smooth S-curve while speed is held low.
  • 3 · Acceleration — once level, acceleration ramps up progressively toward cruise speed.
  • 4 · Approach — nearing the target the drone lessens its acceleration, slows slightly and begins tipping into the dive.
  • 5 · Locked — after target lock it accelerates to maximum velocity and strikes the block.

The speed-profile graph in the telemetry panel plots this live, colour-coded by phase.

Vision seeker

  • Nose camera — a forward-looking 50° camera on the drone's nose, shown live in the corner window (⤢ or V to enlarge).
  • Identification — every frame is scanned for the target; other objects (decoys) are classified and rejected. Hills and trees can hide the target.
  • States — SEARCHING → ACQUIRING → TRACKING → LOCKED. If the target is hidden the tracker coasts for 1.5 s, then re-searches.
  • Steering — cruise flies to the designated coordinates (INS cue). From approach onward the drone steers on the target's pixel offset from the crosshair. VISION STEERING: OFF reverts to coordinate-only guidance so you can compare.
  • Note — the detector is simulated (a hidden render of the scene, with real occlusion) rather than a neural network. Guidance only receives image-plane measurements.

Camera

  • Views — Field, Chase, Side, Top, Front, Back, auto-Orbit, and Target-cam (a telephoto camera at the target tracking the drone in). Keys 1–8.
  • Distance — presets down to 2 m, a slider, mouse wheel, pinch, or + / −.
  • Orbit — drag with mouse/finger or use the arrow keys on top of any view.
  • Attitude — AUTO uses each view's default; LOCK keeps the camera fixed to the airframe's pitch; WORLD keeps it level with the horizon.
  • H hides the interface, Space pauses, R resets the view.
